ABOUT ME

Biography

I am a lecturer in the College of Computer Science and Technology at Huaqiao University. My work explores multimedia content security, artificial intelligence security, digital forensics, and watermarking for deep neural networks and generative models.

I received my Ph.D. in Information and Communication Engineering from Southwest Jiaotong University in 2022, advised by Prof. Hongxia Wang of Sichuan University. From 2019 to 2021, supported by the China Scholarship Council, I conducted joint doctoral research at the University of Siena with IEEE Fellow Prof. Mauro Barni.

Prospective students: Research-minded master’s applicants interested in network and information security are welcome to reach out.

RESEARCH

Research focus

Building practical safeguards for the authenticity, ownership, and forensic traceability of intelligent media.

  • Model and generative watermarking. Robust ownership protection for neural networks, latent diffusion models, and speech synthesis systems.
  • Multimedia content security. Trusted identification, provenance, and tamper-resistant protection across image, audio, video, and 3D media.
  • AI security and digital forensics. Active defense against facial manipulation and forensic techniques for identifying synthetic or malicious media.
